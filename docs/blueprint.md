# **App Name**: The Crochet Studio

## Core Features:

- Hero Section Showcase: A visually compelling hero section featuring the tagline 'The Art of Handcrafted Comfort' and a clear call-to-action button, reflecting the brand's aesthetic.
- Featured Products Gallery: An elegantly structured three-column grid to display key handcrafted products, with high-quality images and brief descriptions.
- Custom Order Section: A dedicated area outlining the process for bespoke crochet orders, including information on personalization and an inquiry interface.
- AI Crochet Idea Tool: An AI-powered tool to assist customers in conceptualizing unique custom crochet designs by suggesting creative ideas based on their input and desired aesthetics.
- Intuitive Site Navigation: A clean and minimalist navigation bar providing easy access to main sections like 'Shop', 'Custom Orders', 'Our Story', and 'Contact'.
- Artistic Visual Accents: Integration of subtle watercolor effects and delicate line art throughout the page to enhance the handcrafted and boutique feel.

## Style Guidelines:

- The palette revolves around sophisticated teal and serene cream tones. The primary color is a deep, muted teal (#1E6B7A), chosen to evoke elegance and natural warmth, making it ideal for impactful elements like the CTA button. Its hue harmonizes with the overarching theme of handcrafted comfort and art.
- The background features a soft, desaturated off-white cream (#F5F8F8). This heavily desaturated hue from the teal spectrum provides an ample 'canvas' for other elements to stand out, adhering to the minimalist and airy aesthetic, and reinforces the feeling of comfort and elegance.
- An accent color of a muted sage-greenish-teal (#6DA698) is used to complement the primary teal. It offers a subtle contrast in brightness and saturation, adding visual interest without overpowering the calm atmosphere and enhancing the handcrafted theme.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif) to achieve a sophisticated, high-end, and elegant aesthetic, complementing the artistic and luxurious feel of the handcrafted items.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for clean readability and a modern, unobtrusive feel, ensuring comfortable legibility while maintaining a contemporary edge.
- Integrate custom delicate line art illustrations and watercolor texture accents throughout the layout to emphasize the artistic, handcrafted, and boutique nature of the brand.
- Embrace an elegant and minimalist layout featuring generous whitespace to enhance visual clarity and provide a premium, uncluttered user experience. Implement the requested three-column grid for product displays and clear sectioning.
- Implement subtle, smooth transition animations for elements such as image loading, navigation interactions, and call-to-action buttons to add a touch of refined interactivity without distracting from the overall aesthetic.
